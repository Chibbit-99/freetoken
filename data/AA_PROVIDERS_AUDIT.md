## Outcome

- **Already catalogued:** Google AI Studio, Groq, SambaNova, Cohere, Mistral, Z.AI, SiliconFlow, Novita, Nebius, Fireworks, Scaleway, Alibaba, Modal, Upstage, Hyperbolic, NVIDIA NIM, Agnes AI, Tencent, plus the card-gated clouds recorded as trials (AWS, Azure).
- **Added this week from the same list:** GMI Cloud (two free endpoints, no card).
- **Nothing else on the AA leaderboard is free.** The rest are GPU clouds and per-token hosts (Crusoe, CoreWeave, Baseten, LithosAI, Supernova, PrimaLabs, Bitdeer, Inco, Parasail, DigitalOcean) or proprietary model owners with no free API (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, DeepSeek, Meta).
- **Borderline:** Together AI is the only large AA provider whose free access is disputed — its own pricing page lists per-token rates only, while several 2026 posts claim a $1–$25 signup credit and '68 free models'. Left out until the vendor states it.
